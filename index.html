Privacy Policy & Client Data Protection

At Shubham Events Planner (spearheaded by Shubham Kr Sharma), preserving client confidentiality and protecting personal information is our sacred commitment. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, safeguard, and manage your data across our luxury event architecture services.

1. Data We Collect

When inquiring about or booking an event, we collect information essential for wedding execution: your name, email address, phone number, destination/city preference, estimated guest count, and tentative budget.

2. Confidentiality & Security

We implement strict encryption protocols and secure storage. High-profile celebrity weddings, industrial galas, and private family celebrations managed by Shubham Events are bound by strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) upon client request.

3. Third-Party Vendor Coordination

To execute royal mandap decor, luxury catering, charter logistics, and artist bookings, relevant event details are shared strictly with verified partner hotels (e.g., Rajasthan heritage palaces, Goa resorts) and master artisans.

4. Your Data Rights

Clients retain full ownership of their personal information. You can inspect, update, or request complete deletion of your booking records at any time through your User Dashboard or by emailing info@shubhamevents.in.

5. Contact & Data Officer

For privacy inquiries or NDA execution, contact our Lead Event Director: Shubham Kr Sharma, C-Scheme, Pink City, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India (+91 98765 43210).
